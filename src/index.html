Email X-Ray

See where that email really came from.

Paste the raw source of a suspicious email. You'll get the path it took across the internet, whether the sender checks out, and where every link actually goes.

How to get the raw source
  • Gmail (web): open the email, click the three-dot menu, choose Show original, then Copy to clipboard.
  • Outlook (web): open the email, click the three-dot menu, then View, then View message source.
  • Apple Mail (Mac): View, then Message, then Raw Source.
  • Yahoo Mail: click More, then View raw message.
  • Outlook (desktop): drag the email from your inbox to your desktop to save it as a .msg file, then drop it here.
  • Most phone mail apps hide this, so a computer is easiest. You can also save the email as a .eml file and open it here.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste leaves this page.

Email X-Ray v1. A scan is a second opinion, not a guarantee. When an email asks for money, passwords, or urgency, contact the sender through a number or site you already trust.
